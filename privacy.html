Website privacy

Updated September 5, 2026.

Information you send us

The callback form asks for your name, phone number, town, and a description of your request. You can also tell us how you heard about us. When you select a particular service before opening the form, that service is included with your request.

We use this information to respond to your enquiry, discuss your pest concerns, and arrange service if you choose to proceed. Please do not send payment card details, account passwords, or other sensitive information through this form.

Services that help this website work

Callback requests are processed through Formspree. Website hosting and delivery providers may process technical information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to deliver and protect the site. Fonts are provided by Google Fonts.

When Google Maps reviews are displayed, this website uses Google Maps Platform. Google may process technical information when its content or reviewer images load. Google Maps features are subject to the Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy. See also Formspree’s privacy policy.

When enabled, our review section also uses SociableKIT to display selected Google reviews. Loading that widget may share technical information, such as your IP address and browser details, with the provider and its content services.

Website analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand website visits, traffic sources, phone-link clicks, and successful callback requests. Google Analytics uses cookies and processes technical and usage information, such as pages viewed, browser and device details, and interactions with the site. Our custom lead events do not include the name, phone number, town, or message you enter in the callback form. Google signals and advertising personalization signals are disabled in our website configuration. Learn more in Google’s privacy policy or use the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Links to other websites

Links to Google, Facebook, and other providers open services operated by those companies. Their privacy policies apply when you use them.

Questions about your information

To ask about information you sent us, or to request a correction or deletion, call Above All Pest Control at 973-728-4455.
